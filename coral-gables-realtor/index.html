Coral Gables Market

A distinctive Miami real estate market

Coral Gables offers a mix of historic homes, renovated residences, luxury estates, condos and waterfront properties. Proximity to Downtown Miami, Coconut Grove, Brickell, the University of Miami and major employment centers continues to make the area attractive to both local and relocating buyers.

Because values can vary significantly by neighborhood, property condition, lot size and location, successful buying or selling requires careful analysis of comparable properties and current market activity.
