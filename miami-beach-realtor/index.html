A Market With Many Micro-Markets

Miami Beach is not one single real estate market

South Beach, Mid-Beach and North Beach can offer very different buying experiences. Even within the same area, a waterfront condo, an Art Deco building, a newer luxury tower and a single-family home may attract completely different buyers.

Location relative to the ocean, Biscayne Bay, major roads, restaurants and commercial areas can all affect pricing and demand.

Buying a Condo

The building matters as much as the unit

HOA & Assessments

Monthly association fees, reserves, insurance costs and current or potential assessments should be reviewed carefully before purchasing.

Building Condition

Age, maintenance history, structural work, renovations and common areas can have a significant impact on ownership costs and resale appeal.

Views & Exposure

Ocean, bay and city views, floor level and unit orientation can create substantial differences in value within the same building.

South Beach, Mid-Beach & North Beach

Choosing the right part of Miami Beach

South Beach offers the most urban and active environment, with a mix of historic buildings, luxury condos, restaurants and nightlife. Buyers looking for walkability and access to the beach often focus here.

Mid-Beach has a different profile, with larger luxury developments, oceanfront towers and quieter residential sections.

North Beach can offer another balance entirely, with a more residential feel in many areas and a mix of condos, smaller buildings and waterfront opportunities.

Waterfront Property

Water access creates another layer of value

Miami Beach waterfront real estate can vary significantly depending on whether the property faces the ocean, Biscayne Bay, a canal or an internal waterway.

For single-family homes, buyers may also consider dockage, bridge clearance, water depth, seawall condition and boating access. These factors can materially influence the value of a waterfront property.

Selling in Miami Beach

Positioning your property against the right competition

A Miami Beach property should not be priced solely by ZIP code or basic square footage. The relevant competition may be other units in the same building, similar waterfront properties or homes with comparable lot and location characteristics.

When preparing a property for sale, I look at active competition, recent sales, building profile, condition, views, carrying costs and how buyers are comparing similar properties in the current market.
