Weston Communities

Community selection is a major part of the purchase

Weston's residential market is heavily influenced by its individual communities. Buyers may encounter different gate structures, amenities, HOA costs, lot sizes, waterfront positions and architectural styles depending on where they search.

That makes choosing the community nearly as important as choosing the individual house.

Buying in Weston

What deserves careful comparison

Community & HOA

Fees, amenities, security and community standards should be evaluated alongside the property's purchase price.

Waterfront & Lot

Lake exposure, privacy, lot position and outdoor living space can create meaningful premiums.

Condition

Renovations, roof, impact protection and major systems can substantially change the economics of two similarly sized homes.

Luxury Homes

Comparable sales need to be selected carefully

At higher price points, differences in community, lot, waterfront exposure, renovation quality and home size become increasingly important.

Using broad citywide averages can obscure those differences. A useful valuation should focus on the segment of the Weston market that actually competes with the property.

Selling in Weston

Buyers compare lifestyle as well as the house

A strong listing strategy should communicate what differentiates the home and the community—whether that is waterfront positioning, privacy, renovations, outdoor space, amenities or location.

Pricing should then be supported by the most relevant recent sales and current competing inventory.
