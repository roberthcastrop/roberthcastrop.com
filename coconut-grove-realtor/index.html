Coconut Grove Lifestyle

A unique part of the Miami market

Coconut Grove is known for its lush residential streets, proximity to Biscayne Bay, marinas, parks, restaurants and established neighborhoods. The area includes everything from historic residences and contemporary homes to luxury condominiums and waterfront estates.

Property values can vary considerably depending on location, lot size, condition, architecture and proximity to the waterfront, making accurate comparable analysis particularly important when buying or selling.
