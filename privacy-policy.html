This Privacy Policy explains how Roberth Castro, REALTOR® with Keller Williams Capital Realty ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information submitted through roberthcastrop.com.

Information We Collect

When you use this website or submit a contact form, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected through this website to respond to inquiries, provide requested real estate information, communicate regarding buying, selling, or investing in real estate, schedule appointments, provide customer service, and send communications where you have provided the appropriate consent.

SMS Communications

If you separately opt in to receive SMS text messages, Roberth Castro, REALTOR® with Keller Williams Capital Realty may send you marketing and promotional text messages relating to real estate products, services, properties, opportunities, appointments, or inquiries.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages or HELP for assistance.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any property, product, or service.

SMS Privacy Notice:

Mobile information, including telephone numbers, SMS opt-in data, consent records, and text messaging originator opt-in information, will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

All categories of information sharing described in this Privacy Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. This information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Telephone Communications

Consent to receive marketing or promotional telephone calls is collected separately from consent to receive SMS text messages.

Providing your telephone number through the website does not by itself constitute consent to receive marketing SMS text messages.

Sharing of Information

We may use service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, process inquiries, maintain communications, or provide requested services. These providers may process information only as necessary to perform services on our behalf.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, safety, or property.

SMS opt-in consent, mobile telephone numbers, and text messaging originator opt-in data will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information. However, no Internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Communication Choices

You may request that we stop marketing communications at any time.

For SMS text messages, reply STOP to opt out. For assistance, reply HELP.

You may also contact us directly to request changes to your communication preferences.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
