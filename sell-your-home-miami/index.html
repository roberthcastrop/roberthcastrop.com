Before You List

What is your Miami property actually worth?

Online estimates can be useful as a starting point, but they cannot fully evaluate renovations, lot characteristics, views, condition, upgrades, building quality, current competition or the motivation of recent sellers.

A useful pricing analysis looks at the properties buyers will realistically compare against yours—not simply the closest homes that recently sold.

That distinction becomes especially important in Miami, where values can change significantly from one street, building or community to another.

Selling Strategy

Four parts of a strong listing strategy

Pricing

We analyze recent comparable sales, active competition, condition and current buyer behavior to establish a realistic market position.

Presentation

Photography, property preparation and the way the home is presented can significantly influence a buyer's first impression.

Exposure

The property should be positioned across the channels where qualified buyers and real estate professionals are actively searching.

Negotiation

Price matters, but so do financing, contingencies, inspection terms, closing dates and the probability that the transaction actually closes.

Buyer Screening

Before accepting an offer, the strength of the buyer's financing or proof of funds should be considered alongside the proposed price.

Closing Management

A signed contract is only the beginning. Inspections, financing, appraisal, title and deadlines must be managed through closing.

Pricing

Overpricing can cost more than it appears

Sellers naturally want the highest possible price. The problem occurs when an asking price moves so far above the market that qualified buyers stop considering the property.

A home can then accumulate days on market, require price reductions and eventually create the impression that something is wrong with the property.

The objective is not to price low. It is to identify the price range that creates the strongest combination of exposure, negotiating leverage and realistic market value.

Offers
