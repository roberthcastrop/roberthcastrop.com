Island Real Estate

Key Biscayne is a small market where location can change everything

Key Biscayne offers a very different residential experience from mainland Miami. The island combines oceanfront condominiums, established single-family neighborhoods, luxury residences and waterfront properties within a relatively compact area.

Because inventory is limited and property types vary significantly, buyers should look closely at building quality, lot characteristics, waterfront exposure, renovation level and long-term carrying costs.

Buying in Key Biscayne

What deserves a closer look

Condo Buildings

Association fees, reserves, assessments, building age, insurance costs, amenities and beach access can materially affect the ownership profile of a condo.

Single-Family Homes

Lot size, location within the island, age of the home, renovation quality and flood considerations can create major differences in value.

Waterfront Property

Bayfront, canal-front and ocean-oriented properties can carry very different premiums depending on view, water access, dockage and overall exposure.

Island Lifestyle

Privacy, beach access and proximity to Miami

Key Biscayne appeals to buyers who want a quieter residential setting while remaining close to Brickell and Downtown Miami.

Access to beaches, parks and waterfront recreation is a major part of the island's appeal. At the same time, the community's access is concentrated through the Rickenbacker Causeway, making location and commuting patterns more relevant here than in many other Miami neighborhoods.

Selling in Key Biscayne

The right comparable is rarely just the closest property

A renovated home on a strong street, an older property with redevelopment potential and a luxury condo with direct water views may all appeal to completely different buyers.

When preparing a Key Biscayne property for sale, pricing should account for property type, building or street, condition, views, lot characteristics, active competition and the specific segment of buyers most likely to consider the property.

Condo vs. House

Two very different ownership experiences

Condos may offer amenities, security and direct beach access, while single-family homes provide more privacy, land and flexibility.

The better choice depends on lifestyle, maintenance expectations, monthly carrying costs and how long the buyer expects to hold the property.
