A Broad Residential Market

The right community depends on what you actually need

Pembroke Pines stretches across a large portion of southwest Broward and offers everything from established neighborhoods to gated communities, townhomes and condominium developments.

Prices and ownership costs can change substantially based on the community, property type, HOA structure and condition.

Buying

Compare the complete property

HOA

Understand monthly fees, amenities, reserves and restrictions before comparing communities solely by purchase price.

Condition

Roof age, impact protection, HVAC and renovations can materially affect the cost of ownership.

Location

Commute, access to major roads and location within Pembroke Pines should be part of the decision.

Selling

Position the home against its real competition

Buyers may compare your home with multiple communities within the same budget. Pricing should therefore account for condition, amenities, HOA expenses, lot, upgrades and competing inventory.

The objective is not simply to be listed—it is to give the buyer a clear reason to choose your property over the alternatives.
