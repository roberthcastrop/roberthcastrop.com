Real Estate in Bay Harbor Islands, Florida

Bay Harbor Islands is a waterfront community situated between the mainland and Miami Beach, immediately west of Bal Harbour and Surfside. Its location provides convenient access to beaches, Bal Harbour Shops, Miami Beach and surrounding South Florida destinations.

The local real estate market includes condominiums, luxury residences and waterfront properties. Values can vary considerably based on location, building, water views, amenities, property condition and recent comparable sales.
